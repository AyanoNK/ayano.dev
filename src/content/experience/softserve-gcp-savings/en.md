## The client and the problem

This was an internal SoftServe initiative. Google Cloud offers Committed Use Discounts: a company commits to a level of usage and pays less for it. Knowing how much to commit to, and how much it would save, takes careful analysis of past spending.

## What I built

I owned the creation of a cost optimization pipeline that predicts the potential savings from purchasing Committed Use Discounts. It uses Google Cloud billing exports and recommendations for the data engineering and the predictive modeling.

## Results

- Identified potential cost reductions of up to 30%.
