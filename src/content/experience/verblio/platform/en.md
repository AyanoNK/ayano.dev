### The client and the problem

Verblio is a US content marketing platform. Its web platform was a decade old when the company moved to a new business model, and the platform needed new features to support it without breaking what customers already used.

### What I did

I maintained and extended the platform, using Ruby on Rails for the backend, Angular for the frontend, PostgreSQL for data storage and Heroku for deployments. I took part in designing, implementing and testing new features across their whole lifecycle, and gave technical support on both new and legacy features.

- **New business line.** I worked with product managers to bring customers into the new in-app business model, and moved 70% of customers into it.
- **Fraud detection.** I implemented a fraud detection system that combines behavioral and rule-based detection, which reduced active fraudulent accounts by 95%.
- **Workflow automation.** I automated a platform workflow so administrators no longer had to step in for specific case scenarios, cutting that access by 50%.
- **Documentation.** I documented the complex processes behind price lists and the lifecycle of in-app credits, which cut questions to the development team by 50%.
- **Legacy code.** I delivered and reviewed new solutions in the decade-old codebase without breaking any existing feature.

### Results

- 70% of customers moved into the new business line.
- 95% fewer active fraudulent accounts.
- 50% fewer questions to the development team, and 50% less administrator access needed in specific cases.
