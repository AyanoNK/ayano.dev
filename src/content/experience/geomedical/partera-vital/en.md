### The client and the problem

Partera Vital is a public health project by UNFPA Colombia and DANE, Colombia's national statistics department. It connects traditional midwives in Chocó, Colombia with health systems and vital statistics registration.

Chocó has Afro-Colombian and indigenous communities where maternal mortality can triple national figures and State presence is scarce.

### What I built

I led the ground-up development of the Partera Vital MVP. I used React Native for the mobile app the midwives use, Flask for the backend that receives the records, and Google Cloud for the infrastructure.

Internet connectivity in the region is so intermittent that it is unserviceable for the most part. I engineered offline-first data synchronization, so the app stores records on the phone and sends them when a connection appears.

The lack of technological resources at remote locations also caused data veracity challenges, which I tackled as part of the project.

### Results

- The complete application was delivered in 3 months.
- Birth records and maternal health alerts now reach public entities from communities where the State has almost no presence.
- The application remains in use today.
