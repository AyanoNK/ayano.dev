## The client and the problem

This was an internal SoftServe initiative to build a platform that can generate and coordinate autonomous agents. The focus was on explainability, modularity and managing the full lifecycle of each AI agent.

## What I built

I developed the AI orchestrator platform. To control its cost, I measured input and output token consumption across the orchestrator and its subagents, then introduced skills and caching so the agents send fewer tokens for the same work.

## Results

- Token usage reduced by 15%.
