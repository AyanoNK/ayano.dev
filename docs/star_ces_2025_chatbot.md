# STAR: AI Chatbot Featured at CES 2025

## Situation

The client, a company in the online surveys and data analysis industry, needed a way for users to query complex datasets without writing SQL or understanding database schemas. Non-technical users were unable to access valuable data insights, creating a bottleneck between the data and the people who needed it.

## Task

Lead a team to build a greenfield AI chatbot from scratch that dynamically generates and executes database queries to answer user questions in natural language — making data accessible to anyone, regardless of technical skill.

## Action

Led a 6-person team through the full development lifecycle of the chatbot. Designed the architecture for dynamic query generation and execution, enabling the system to interpret natural language questions, translate them into database queries, run them, and return human-readable answers. Coordinated cross-functional collaboration across engineering and product teams to deliver the product on time.

## Result

The AI chatbot was selected to be **featured at CES 2025**, one of the world's largest technology expos — validating the product's innovation, technical quality, and market readiness. The product eliminated the technical barrier between users and their data.
